## Fees (2026/2027)

Tuition (₦80,000) and administrative charge (₦15,000) are the same for every
class. Books & stationeries vary by class. Admission form and uniform are for
**new scholars only** (marked \*).

- Books & stationeries: paid **once a session**.
- Girls' uniform is ₦10,000 more than boys' because of the hijab.
- Billing period: "Some are per term, some are per session." Only books are
  confirmed as once a session. See [Open questions](#open-questions).
- Sibling discount: none yet.
- Scholarships: none for now.

**Founding story:**
Fountain-I Academy, Ilorin, was founded in 2026 out of a vision to provide an
educational experience where academic excellence, authentic Islamic Tarbiyah
and character development flow together.

The Academy was established by Dr. Abdullahi A. Taoheed, Hajiah Jelilat A.

and technological innovations.
